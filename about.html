About

I am the kind of person who'd rather say "I don't know" and mean it than pretend otherwise — starting from zero feels more honest than faking eighty percent. I care more about figuring out what the right thing is than about how quickly it can be built, and I enjoy a good argument, as long as it's about the idea and not the person. I'm direct about what I think and patient with the people around me, sometimes more patient than I should be. My curiosity doesn't really switch off; it just quietly asks one more question, usually right when everyone else was ready to move on.
